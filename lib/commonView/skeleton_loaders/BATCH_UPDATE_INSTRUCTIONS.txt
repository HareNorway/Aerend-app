WHY 1500ms?
==============================================================================

The new period of 1500ms provides:
- Smooth, non-blinking appearance
- Wave-like shimmer effect that feels natural
- Consistent with modern UX patterns (Material Design, iOS)
- Optimal balance between noticeable feedback and non-intrusive

Previous 800ms was too fast (caused blinking effect).
